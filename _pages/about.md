Welcome to my website! 

I read Natural Sciences (B.A. (Hons) and M.Sci.) at the University of Cambridge, graduating with a First Class in 2025. During my studies, I became interested in applying fluid dynamics, mineral physics and numerical modelling to predict the futures of global ice sheets and their implications for sea level rise. I am currently a visitor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Manchester, undertaking research with Dr Ashleigh Hutchinson into the behaviour of Saffman-Taylor instabilities in shear-thinning fluids. A report of some of the research I undertook is available <a href="/pdfs/Saffman_Taylor_report_rjc.pdf">here</a>.

During my master's degree, I used geophysical inversion to improve melt rate estimates in the grounding zones of Antarctic ice shelves. My thesis is available <a href="/pdfs/Part_III_Dissertation_rjc213.pdf">here</a>.

During my bachelor's degree, I carried out an independent research project investigating the aqueous geochemistry of the étang de Thau, southern France. My thesis is available <a href="/pdfs/Part II Report Reefe Conley.pdf">here</a>.
